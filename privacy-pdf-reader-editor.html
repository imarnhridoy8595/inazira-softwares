Privacy Policy

Inazira PDF Reader & Editor

How the app handles your information.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Inazira PDF Reader & Editor ("the app") is published by INAZIRA (https://softwares.inazira.com).

The short version

The app works completely offline. It does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not have permission to access the internet.

Your files

  • You choose which PDF files and pictures the app opens, using Android's built-in file picker (or by opening or sharing a file to the app from another app).
  • Files are read and edited only on your device. To work on a file, the app makes a temporary copy in its own private storage on your phone. These temporary copies are removed automatically from time to time and when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Edited files are saved only where you choose (the original file, or a new file or folder you pick). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To make the app convenient, it keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage on your phone: the list of recently opened files (file name, size, a small preview picture of the first page, a reference to the file and the last page you viewed), and your display setting (night mode). This information never leaves your device. You can remove an item from the recent list with its "x" button, and you can erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app asks for no Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos library or the internet.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the PDF is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet (for example WhatsApp or Gmail). That happens only when you ask for it, and what that other app does with the file is governed by its own privacy policy.

Third-party software

The app includes open-source components (such as pdf.js, pdf-lib, fontkit, Capacitor and the Hind Siliguri font). They run entirely inside the app on your phone and do not send any data anywhere. Their licences are listed in the app under About & licences.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Security

Because your files and settings stay on your device, they are protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and storage protection). Please keep your phone secured.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed.
