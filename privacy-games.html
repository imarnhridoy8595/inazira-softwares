Privacy Policy

Inazira Games

How the app handles your information.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Inazira Games ("the app") is a collection of offline games (Ludo, Chess, 29 and Bridge) published by INAZIRA (https://inazira.com).

The short version

The app works completely offline. It does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools.

Information stored on your device

Game settings and saved progress (for example a Bridge rubber score or sound/speed settings) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app asks for no Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos library or the internet.

Sharing

There is no sharing option in any part of the games.

Third-party software

The app includes no third party components.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Because your files and settings stay on your device, they are protected by your phone's own security (screen lock and storage protection). Please keep your phone secured.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: inazira.official@gmail.com or https://softwares.inazira.com
